# Question bank

Every question below is something a client or the owner saw after automated checks had passed the piece. Pick the
ones that apply; put the person's reference sheet beside each face. S = stills, T = takes, C = cuts.
"Fails" lists the answers that stop the piece.

## Faces
- F1 [S T C] Beside the reference sheet, is each face the same person? same | a lookalike | someone else. Fails:
  lookalike, someone else.
- F2 [S T C] Hair as the reference: length, colour, cut? same | different.
- F3 [S T C] Does anyone look tired, tipsy, ill, older or not themselves? no | yes.
- F4 [T C] Eyes: into the lens, a wide-open stare, rolled up, another colour or shape than the same person's other
  crops? no | yes.
- F5 [T C] Does a face melt, change shape or become another person inside a shot or between shots? no | yes.
- F6 [T C] An expression the script doesn't want: a fixed grin, a smirk, a smile where they should be tense? no | yes.

## People and the set (plus the set's own fact questions: scene-set `ask`)
- P1 [S T C] Heights as the cards give them; seated, the taller one's head higher? as the cards | wrong.
- P2 [S T C] Does anyone half-sit, stand where they should sit, or sit somewhere else? no | yes.
- P3 [S T C] At the frame edges: a stranger, a head, a shoulder, an arm that belongs to nobody? no | yes.
- P4 [C] The same room in every shot: walls, window, table axis, light side? same | different.
- P5 [S T C] Does everyone look where the set says, nobody into the lens? yes | no.
- P6 [S] All stills of one person side by side: the same person, the same view behind them (time of day, lit
  windows, colour)? same | different.

## Hands and props
- H1 [S T C] Extra or missing fingers, fused hands, a hand nobody owns? no | yes.
- H2 [S T C] Does anyone hold another person's hand or arm where the script doesn't ask for it? no | yes.
- H3 [T C] A hand that works an object: describe which hand holds it and what moves the moving part, first. Fails:
  "moves by itself", "stuck to the finger", "can't tell", or not how people do it.
- H4 [T C] Does any object appear, vanish, change or move with no hand moving it? Is every prop the script needs
  there? no | yes.
- H5 [S T C] Anything thrown, tossed or frozen in the air? no | yes.
- H6 [T C] Props on people the same in every shot: glasses (both rims, bridge, temples), jewellery, collar, mic,
  earbuds? same | changed.
- H7 [T C] Does an object pass between people on camera or across a cut? no | yes.
- H8 [S T] Where a still was edited or composited: a seam, a halo, a dark line, a smeared patch? no | yes.
- H9 [T C] Hands clasped, steepled or fisted where the character card says open? never | sometimes (times) | mostly.

## The product
- C1 [S T C] Size against the real product pasted at true size beside it: bigger | same (within a tenth) | smaller |
  can't tell. Fails: bigger, smaller.
- C2 [S T C] Logo or crest as the real one? same | different | can't see. Fails: different.
- C3 [S T C] Lettering: same words | garbled | hidden. Fails: garbled.
- C4 [S T C] Shape and colour as the real product? same | different.
- C5 [S T C] Does it look pasted, plastic or flat, or lit unlike the room? no | yes.
- C6 [T C] While it moves, does the label swim, smear or redraw? no | yes.
- C7 [C] The same product in consecutive shots: same size, colour and side? yes | no.

## Clothes and text
- B1 [S T C] Every logo on clothes: exactly the real mark | another emblem | letters | too small to tell. Fails:
  another emblem, letters.
- B2 [S T C] Each garment in the set's colour, the same in every shot? yes | no.
- B3 [S T C] Any text the model made: on a screen, a shirt, a wall? no | yes.

## Acting and motion
- A1 [T C] Does the action match the line? Is anyone frozen or robotic for more than ~2 s? yes | no.
- A2 [T C] Does a gesture read wrong or artificial (a posed cheer, a peek, crying for laughing)? no | yes.
- A3 [T C] Does anyone do something the script doesn't have: pick up, toss, clink, enter? no | yes.
- A4 [T C] Real-speed movement, no reversal (down, up, down) and no restart of an action? yes | no.
- A5 [T C] Mouths closed in silent shots and at every cut; in dialogue only the speaker's moves? yes | no.
- A6 [T] The background that must move (sea, screen, crowd) moves? yes | no.

## Sound on picture (give the reviewer a sync table: time, what is heard, the frame at that time)
- S1 [C] Every effect plays over the shot that shows its source? match | loose | mismatch. Fails: mismatch.
- S2 [C] A sound cue the script names has its event on screen then? yes | no.
- S3 [C] Every VO line plays over the shot it's about, with pauses between lines? yes | no.
- S4 [C] Each voice is the cast's voice, with the right accent? yes | no.
- S5 [C] A word cut, doubled, mumbled or blurred, a laugh after a line, a word that sounds like another person?
  no | yes.

## Edit and story
- E1 [C] Retell the story from the frame sheet and the timed lines: what happens, who says what, what confuses you.
- E2 [C] Does each reply start on the replier's face or after a beat of ≥ 0.5 s? yes | no.
- E3 [C] Do inserts show who acts (a face, over a shoulder), not anonymous hands? yes | no.
- E4 [C] Would a stranger scrolling past stop in the first 2 seconds? Name what stops them, or say nothing does.
